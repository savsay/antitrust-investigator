# Privacy policy — Antitrust Investigator

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Antitrust Investigator (plugin name `rekabet-ihlali-inceleme`) is a set of instructions, reference catalogues and small Python scripts that run inside Claude. The publisher operates no server and receives no data.

## What the plugin processes

- **Your documents.** When you ask for a review, Claude reads the emails, chats, documents, spreadsheets and exports that you or your organisation point it to, within the scope you authorise. Processing happens in your Claude session under your Claude plan's own data terms.
- **Bundled scripts.** The scripts read those files and write CSV/JSON output to your working folder. They make no network calls and read no credentials or environment variables.
- **Web lookups.** When a legal source is missing or out of date, Claude may search official websites (for example rekabet.gov.tr, mevzuat.gov.tr, europa.eu, justice.gov, gov.uk, oecd.org). The skill instructs Claude to put only legal topics in those queries, never personal names, project names, customers or amounts.

## What the plugin does not do

- It does not send your data to the publisher or to any third party.
- It does not store data outside your session and the output files you ask for.
- It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

## Personal data and your responsibilities

Reviewing employee communications involves personal data. You are the controller of that processing and need a lawful basis, proportionality and employee notice under KVKK, GDPR and local labour law. The skill asks for a documented mandate and scope before reading content, skips out-of-scope personal content and keeps whistleblower identities out of reports, but it cannot provide the legal basis for you.

The plugin is intended for professional use by adults and is not directed at people under 18.
